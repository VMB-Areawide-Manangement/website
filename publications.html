Biological Control of Vine Mealybug: Current Status and Future Prospects

Daane, K.M., et al. (2022). Journal of Applied Entomology

This paper reviews the current status of biological control programs for vine mealybug in California vineyards, with emphasis on the use of parasitoids and predators.

Area-Wide Management of Vine Mealybug in California Vineyards

Daane, K.M., et al. (2020). Pest Management Science

Evaluation of an area-wide management program for vine mealybug in California's Central Coast vineyards, demonstrating reduced pest populations and economic benefits.

Seasonal Phenology and Management of Vine Mealybug in Vineyards

Daane, K.M., et al. (2019). Journal of Economic Entomology

Detailed study of vine mealybug seasonal development patterns and implications for timing control measures in commercial vineyards.

Read More

Comparative Life History Parameters of Vine Mealybug Populations

Daane, K.M., et al. (2018). Environmental Entomology

Examination of biological differences between vine mealybug populations from different grape-growing regions in California.

Read More

Impact of Ant Control on Vine Mealybug Biological Control

Daane, K.M., et al. (2017). Biological Control

Study demonstrating how Argentine ant control can enhance the effectiveness of natural enemies against vine mealybug in vineyards.

Evaluation of New Insecticides for Vine Mealybug Control

Daane, K.M., et al. (2016). Pest Management Science

Field trials assessing the efficacy of newer reduced-risk insecticides against vine mealybug in commercial vineyards.

Vine Mealybug Transmission of Grapevine Leafroll Viruses

Daane, K.M., et al. (2015). Plant Disease

Research on the role of vine mealybug as a vector of grapevine leafroll-associated viruses in California vineyards.
